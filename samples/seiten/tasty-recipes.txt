Lemon Garlic Roasted Potatoes
Author: Jane Miller
Total Time: 45 minutes
Yield: 4 servings 1x
Diet: Vegetarian
Print Recipe
Pin Recipe
Description
Crispy potatoes with lemon and garlic, ready in under an hour.
Ingredients

2 lbs baby potatoes, halved
3 tablespoons olive oil
4 cloves garlic, minced
1 teaspoon salt
1/2 teaspoon black pepper
1 lemon, zested and juiced
Cook Mode
Prevent your screen from going dark
Instructions
Preheat the oven to 425°F.
Toss the potatoes with the olive oil, garlic, salt and pepper in a large bowl.
Spread the potatoes on a baking sheet and roast for 30-35 minutes, until golden.
Toss with the lemon and serve.
Notes
Leftovers keep for 3 days in the fridge.
