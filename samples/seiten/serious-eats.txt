Crispy Roast Potatoes
Why It Works
- Boiling the potatoes first roughens their surface for extra crunch.
- Shaking them in the pot creates a starchy layer that browns in the oven.
Prep 15 mins
Cook 60 mins
Total 75 mins
Serves 6 servings
Ingredients
2 kg potatoes, peeled and cut into chunks
1 tbsp salt
5 tbsp olive oil
2 sprigs rosemary
Directions
1. Preheat the oven to 230°C.
2. Boil the potatoes in salted water for 10 minutes and drain.
Photographer / Studio
3. Toss the potatoes with the olive oil and rosemary and spread on a baking sheet.
4. Roast for 50 minutes until crisp, then serve.
Special Equipment
Rimmed baking sheet
Notes
The potatoes can be boiled a day ahead.
Make-Ahead and Storage
Keep leftovers in the fridge for up to 3 days.
