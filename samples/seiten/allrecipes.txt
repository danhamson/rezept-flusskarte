Original recipe (1X) yields 8 servings
1 ½ cups all-purpose flour
3 teaspoons baking powder
1 tablespoon sugar
¼ teaspoon salt
1 ¼ cups milk
1 large egg
3 tablespoons butter, melted
Directions
Step 1
Whisk the flour, baking powder, sugar and salt in a large bowl.
Kitchen Studio / Photographer
Step 2
Add the milk, egg and melted butter and stir until smooth.
Step 3
Heat a lightly oiled griddle over medium-high heat. Pour the batter onto the griddle and cook for 2 to 3 minutes until bubbles form. Flip and cook until browned on the other side.
