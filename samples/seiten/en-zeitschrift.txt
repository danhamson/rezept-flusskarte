Easy veggie chilli
Prep: 15 mins
Cook: 1 hr and 5 mins
Easy
Serves 4
A star rating of 4.6 out of 5.120 ratingsRate
A smoky bean chilli that keeps well in the fridge for a few days.
Freezable
Print
Ingredients
Nutrition
Units:Metric
US
For the chilli
1 tbsp olive oil
1 onion finely chopped
2 garlic cloves crushed
1 red pepper deseeded and chopped
1 tsp ground cumin
2 x 400g tins chopped tomatoes
400g tin kidney beans drained
To serve
150g rice

kcal 412
fat 6g
Method
step 1
Heat the oil in a large pan. Add the onion and cook for 5 mins until softened.
step 2
Stir in the garlic, pepper and cumin and cook for 2 mins more.
step 3
Add the tomatoes and beans, then simmer for 45 mins until thickened.
step 4
Cook the rice following the pack instructions and serve with the chilli.
